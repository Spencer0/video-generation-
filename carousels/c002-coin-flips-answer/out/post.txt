WHY THE PINNED COMMENT IS NOT "FLIP 20 TIMES AND COUNT":
In one long run of flips HH and HT each show up equally often (every adjacent pair is HH or HT with chance 1/4). Counting them would 'disprove' us. What differs is the wait for the FIRST one, so viewers have to replay each game from scratch. 5 games each is noisy but usually shows it; HH's spread is wide (median 4, mean 6).

REPLY TO WHY COMMENTS:
From "holding an H": in HT, another H keeps you holding an H, so you only ever wait for a T. In HH, a T throws you back to the start. Expected flips E from start: HT: E = 2 (to get an H) + 2 (to get a T) = 4. HH: E = 1 + E1/2 + E/2 and E1 = 1 + E/2 give E = 6.
